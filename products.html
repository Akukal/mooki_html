What flavors are available?

Mooki comes in four delicious flavors: Choco Blast, Strawberry Pop, Creamy Vanilla, and Banana Bounce. Each one brings its own unique taste to make your day a little sweeter!

What makes Mooki special?

Mooki brings together delicious milk flavors and convenient UHT packaging, making it an easy and enjoyable companion for your everyday activities.

What is UHT milk?

UHT stands for Ultra-High Temperature. It's a milk processing method that uses high temperatures for a short time to help eliminate harmful microorganisms and extend shelf life.

Does Mooki need to be refrigerated?

Nope! Unopened Mooki can be stored at room temperature in a cool, dry place. Once opened, keep it refrigerated and consume according to the packaging instructions.

Is Mooki suitable for children?

Mooki is designed to be a fun milk companion. However, always check the nutritional information and ingredients to make sure it suits your child's dietary needs.

How long does Mooki last?

Unopened Mooki can last up to 10 months thanks to our Flash UHT Seal process. Always check the expiration date printed on the pack!
